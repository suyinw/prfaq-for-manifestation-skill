# Personal PRFAQ — Template

Fill in the brackets. Delete the guidance in _italics_ as you go. Write the
press release in the **present or past tense**, as if the date has already
arrived and this is all true.

---

## PART 1 — THE PRESS RELEASE

> _Target: about one page. Vivid, specific, written from your future._

**PERSONAL PRFAQ FOR: [preferred name]**

**FOR RELEASE: [target date — e.g. September 2027]**

### [Headline: the change, stated as a finished fact and tied to the person — one line]
_e.g. "Maya ran her first marathon — and actually enjoyed the training."_

#### [Subheadline: who this is for and the single biggest benefit — one line]
_e.g. "How a burned-out desk worker became someone who trusts their own body
again — and showed their kids what starting over looks like."_

**[Your city], [target date]** — [Summary paragraph. Open with where you are and
when. In a few plain sentences, describe what is now true that wasn't before, and
why it matters most.]

**The gap.** [Write from who you are TODAY. What was missing, stuck, or painful?
What was it costing you? How did you cope with or work around it before, and what
had you already tried that fell short?]

**The change.** [Who did you become, and how? Describe the concrete habits,
routines, systems, decisions, and support that made this real. Make sure this
directly answers "the gap" above. Add a second paragraph if the change is layered.]

> "[Quote from your future self — what it honestly feels like to be here now.]"

> "[Quote from someone whose life your change touched — a partner, friend, child,
> colleague, someone you went on to help.]"

**Getting started was simple.** [Name the first small, unglamorous step you took
to begin — and, honestly, what made it possible to take it.]

## PART 2 — THE FAQ

### External FAQ
> _What the people around you would ask or notice._

**Q: What would surprise people most about this change?**
[Answer]

**Q: How did you actually do it?**
[Answer]

**Q: What would a skeptic say — and what's your honest response?**
[Answer]

**Q: What would you tell someone who wants to do the same thing?**
[Answer]

### Internal FAQ
> _The honest questions to yourself. This is the most important part. Do not fill
> it only with encouraging answers._

**Q: What has to be true for this to actually happen?**
[Answer — the real conditions.]

**Q: What is the hardest problem you'll have to solve?**
[Answer]

**Q: What are you going to have to give up, say no to, or let go of?**
[Answer]

**Q: What has stopped you before — and what is genuinely different this time?**
[Answer]

**Q: When you hit a setback or fall off (you will), how will you get back on?**
[Answer]

**Q: What are the top three reasons this might NOT happen?**
1. [Reason]
2. [Reason]
3. [Reason]

**Q: What's the smallest version of this you could commit to that would still be
real progress?**
[Answer]

**Q: Is this genuinely the life YOU want — or someone else's idea of success?
How do you know?**
[Answer]
